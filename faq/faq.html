Frequently Asked Questions

What does a hydroponics marketing and design agency do?

A hydroponics agency handles the creative and marketing work for grow, plant nutrition and cannabis brands: product label design, packaging, logos and branding, websites and e-commerce, posters and ads, brochures, catalogues and price lists. We Know Hydro has 20 years of experience in the hydro and cannabis industry, so you don't have to explain your market to us.

Do you design product labels for nutrients, fertilisers and grow products?

Yes. Product labels are what we are best known for. We design bold, eye-catching labels that stand out on shelf and online, for plant nutrients, fertilisers and additives. Past work includes label ranges for Mother Pukka Nutrients and brand and packaging work for Shogun Fertilisers and Zip-Zag.

Can you design branding and packaging for cannabis and counterculture brands?

Yes. We work with medical marijuana, grower and counterculture brands, creating distinctive logos, complete brand identities and packaging. Because we already understand the industry's style and audience, briefs move faster and the results feel authentic rather than generic.

Do you build websites and online stores for hydroponics businesses?

Yes. We build high-performing websites and online stores, including Shopify, that look great and are designed to drive sales. We can also keep your website, social and print material consistent with your brand.

Can you make AI-generated artwork print ready?

Yes. AI can get you about 90% of the way there, and we take it the rest of the way. We refine details, correct inaccuracies, remove obvious AI artefacts and rebuild weak areas, so AI-generated artwork becomes a high-quality asset ready for print, packaging and production. For Zip-Zag, we updated an AI-generated bag design to match the real packaging.

What design work do you do besides labels and websites?

We also design posters and ads, brochures and leaflets, catalogues and price lists, social content, exhibition graphics and vehicle wraps. These are available as standalone projects or together as part of a full brand rollout.

How do you decide the scope of a design project?

We scope by ambition and the time a project needs. Our levels run from Simple to Technical, Complex, Specialist and Beast. Quick, focused jobs need less time, while deeper projects need exploration and iteration. The more time invested, the more developed the ideas and the finish, so the time matches the goal.

Do you offer ongoing design on a monthly retainer?

Yes. With a retainer you set a monthly budget and we handle the rest, so you have an experienced hydroponics design team available without the hassle of briefing new suppliers each time.

Do you work with brands outside the UK?

Yes. We Know Hydro is a global creative and marketing agency for hydroponics, cannabis and counterculture brands, and we work with clients remotely.

Use the contact page to tell us about your brand and what you need, such as a label, rebrand, website or retainer. We'll come back to you about the scope and next steps.
